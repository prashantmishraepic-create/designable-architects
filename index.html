About Us

With deep roots in Lucknow's rich cultural heritage and a finger on the pulse of global design trends, we create spaces that are both timeless and modern, functional and beautiful.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process.

What services does THE DESIGNABLE offer?
We offer comprehensive interior design services including residential interiors, commercial spaces, modular kitchens, furniture design, 3D visualization, interior decoration, and complete turnkey solutions from concept to installation.
How long does a typical interior design project take?
Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A single room design typically takes 2-4 weeks, while complete home interiors may take 2-4 months. We provide detailed timelines during our initial consultation and keep you updated throughout the process.
What is the cost of interior design services in Lucknow?
Our pricing is transparent and customized based on your requirements, space size, materials, and finishes. For a 2BHK apartment, projects typically range from ₹8-12 lakhs, while 3BHK apartments range from ₹12-18 lakhs. We offer free consultations to provide accurate estimates.
Do you provide 3D visualizations before starting work?
Yes! We create detailed 3D renderings and walkthroughs so you can visualize your space before any work begins. This allows you to make informed decisions about layouts, colors, materials, and finishes, ensuring complete satisfaction with the final design.
What areas do you serve in Lucknow?
We serve all areas of Lucknow including Gomti Nagar, Vibhuti Khand, Indira Nagar, Aliganj, Hazratganj, Alambagh, and surrounding regions. Our studio is conveniently located in Gomti Nagar for easy consultations.
Do you handle furniture manufacturing and installation?
Absolutely! We provide complete turnkey solutions including custom furniture manufacturing, modular kitchen installation, electrical work, painting, flooring, and all civil work. Our team manages everything from start to finish, ensuring quality and timely delivery.
Can you work within my budget?
Yes, we work with various budgets and offer flexible solutions. During our consultation, we discuss your requirements and budget to create a customized plan that delivers maximum value. We're transparent about costs and help you make informed choices about materials and finishes.
What makes THE DESIGNABLE different from other designers?
Our unique blend of traditional Lucknow craftsmanship with contemporary design, personalized service, transparent pricing, and complete project management sets us apart. We focus on creating timeless spaces that reflect your personality while ensuring functionality and quality in every detail.
